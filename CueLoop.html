About CueLoop

Built for rehearsing movement, not managing music files.

CueLoop is intentionally lightweight. Your song is loaded locally in your browser and is not uploaded by the app. Project exports save your cue names, notes, timestamps, BPM, and first-beat setting, but do not include the audio itself. CueLoop was created and designed by Erin H. Courville to solve a problem she encountered while choreographing an aerial performance: keeping track of what comes next while rehearsing to music. The application was developed collaboratively using ChatGPT by OpenAI for AI-assisted coding, with Erin directing the design, features, testing, and iterative development.
