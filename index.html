About CueLoop

Built for rehearsing movement, not managing music files.

CueLoop is intentionally lightweight. Your song is loaded locally in your browser and is not uploaded by the app. Project exports save your cue names, notes, timestamps, BPM, and first-beat setting, but do not include the audio itself.
